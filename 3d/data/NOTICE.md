city.json is a derived database made from the Geofabrik Taiwan extract by tools/build/build_city.py (buildings, roads,
road names, rail, water, green areas and labels, simplified and rescaled for the 3D game). As a derived database it
is also under ODbL 1.0, with the same attribution. The parts added for 2033 (park, AI cluster, homes and avenues under
"future") are this project's own illustration of the plan, not OpenStreetMap data.
